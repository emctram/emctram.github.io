Locations

All Souls College

High Street, Oxford, OX1 4AL

All Souls College is on the High Street, beside the University Church and close to Catte Street. If arriving by bus, use one of the High Street stops: Queen's Lane, Brasenose College or High Street.

Please arrive at the Porters' Lodge, where the porters will be able to direct you to the Chapel.

East Oxford Community Centre

Princes Street, Oxford, OX4 1DD

East Oxford Community Centre is at the junction of Princes Street and Cowley Road. The main entrance is on Princes Street.

If arriving by bus, the closest stop is James Street East.

Travel to Oxford

Oxford is well connected by train, bus and coach. Driving into the centre can be difficult, so we recommend public transport or Park & Ride where possible.

Bus and coach

Oxford is well connected regionally and nationally by bus and coach. Long-distance services include FlixBus and National Express. From London, Stagecoach's Oxford Tube runs 24 hours a day.

Car and parking

If travelling by car, we strongly recommend using one of Oxford's Park & Ride facilities. Park & Ride services stop close to both venues.

Central parking is limited. Nearby options include:

  • Westgate Centre car park - approximately 16 minutes' walk from All Souls and 33 minutes from East Oxford Community Centre.
  • Worcester Street car park - approximately 11 minutes' walk from All Souls and 29 minutes from East Oxford Community Centre.
  • St Clement's car park - approximately 11 minutes' walk from All Souls and 7 minutes from East Oxford Community Centre.
  • Union Street car park - approximately 19 minutes' walk from All Souls and 2 minutes from East Oxford Community Centre.

Some additional, relatively expensive metered on-street parking is available in the city centre.

Oxford currently operates a congestion-charge scheme. Please check the council's information before travelling.
Train

Frequent direct services run from London Paddington and Birmingham New Street. Oxford's main station is on Park End Street, near the Botley Road bridge, approximately 18 minutes' walk from All Souls College and 35 minutes from East Oxford Community Centre. Oxford Parkway is the city's second station and is in north Oxford.

Bike

If you are making some or all of your journey by bike, please let us know. We can arrange secure storage at the college, where it is less likely to be stolen. We recommend that you do not leave your bike at the railway station.

Accommodation

Oxford has a wide range of hotels, hostels and short-term rental accommodation. As the wedding falls outside full term, some colleges may also have rooms available.

If you are having difficulty finding somewhere suitable, please let us know and we will do our best to help.

Dress

Please wear whatever makes you feel comfortable and celebratory. Comfortable shoes, or a spare pair, will be useful for the ceilidh.

Accessibility

If you have any access needs, please let us know through the RSVP form or contact us directly so that we can help with arrangements.

All Souls College

All Souls consists of historic buildings that were not designed with modern accessibility in mind. The spaces we will use are on the ground floor, although some are raised by a small number of steps.

The University's All Souls access guide includes photographs, measurements and detailed information for individual areas.

East Oxford Community Centre

The building has street-level ramp access. We will use rooms on the ground and first floors, and the first floor can be reached using an accessible lift suitable for wheelchairs.

Blue Badge parking and the congestion charge: on-street Blue Badge parking is available around the city centre, including Broad Street near All Souls and Princes Street or Cowley Road near East Oxford Community Centre. Blue Badge holders are exempt from the congestion charge, but a permit is required. Details are available on the council website.
